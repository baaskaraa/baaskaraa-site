From Avvaiyar's verse to creation
Vinayagar Agaval
விநாயகர் அகவல்

A classical Tamil verse about the inward path, taking form from the artist's own sketch.

Avvaiyar's Vinayagar Agaval describes the journey of energy up the spine, from the base to the crown. The sketch, drawn by hand on 3 February 2020, gives it a body: a gold axis rising straight through the centre, silver and copper lines winding around it to form the trunk, the sacred geometry of the Sri Chakra at its base, and Avvaiyar herself, small, at the foot of it all.

From that one drawing, the work grows in seven stages:

01 The Word: the verse, read and heard
02 The Line: the drawing
03 The Image: the painting
04 The Object: the sculpture in metal
05 The Space: an installation to walk around
06 The Experience: sound, light, stillness
07 Digital / VR: a journey you can walk through

The institution · since 1850

Since 1850. India's oldest art school.

The Government College of Arts and Crafts, Chennai: the school of K.C.S. Paniker and the Madras Art Movement, and of the founders of Cholamandal Artists' Village. Baskar studied there among the masters of his time: Bachelor of Fine Arts, First Class with Distinction, 1994–1999, then the Master of Fine Arts, until the digital age called in 2000.

On life

Understanding is all. Expression is life. Expression alone doesn't become life. If you do not understand completely, you will try to fill the missing part through various actions — living like this and living like that — which may look like life.

— baaskaraa
On art

Understanding is all. Expression is art. Expression alone doesn't become art. If you do not understand completely, you will try to fill the missing part through various actions — doing like this and doing like that — which may look like art.

— baaskaraa
Statement II

I think in the digital. I reach to make it physical. Between the two — a loss, unnamed. What stands before you is unfinished. It is reaching toward wholeness. When it arrives there, you and the work become one.

— baaskaraa
Statement I

This world searches for artists. But those who step forward are imitators, recreators, careful copiers, shameless self-promoters, and those who hunger only for success.

— baaskaraa
